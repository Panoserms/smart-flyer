Is this pair a good cross-sell bundle?

Label the relation between the two products, as a retail category manager would, for one decision: should the pair go on a flyer as “buy product 1, get product 2 at 20 % off” (or the other way round)? Judge from the names only, don’t look anything up. The order doesn’t matter.

Labels

Complement: different kinds of product that go together: used or eaten together, same recipe, task or occasion, or parts made to be combined. Test: would a shopper add B in addition to A, because of A? Tennis racket + balls; pasta + pasta sauce; party plates + party napkins.

Substitute: the same kind of product, differing in colour, pattern, flavour, size, pack, brand or a near-equivalent type. A discount would shift sales from one to the other. Shirt in red + shirt in blue; apple juice + orange juice; large + small candle; two card games.

Not a bundle: (a) at least one line is not a product for sale: postage, fee, deposit, voucher, till carrier bag, manual adjustment; or (b) two products of different kinds with no specific link. Garden trowel + birthday card; lightbulbs + cat food.

Borderline rules

  1. Same collection, different function (matching teapot + teacup): complement.
  2. Same function, different design (lunch bag apple + lunch bag cars): substitute, even if some buy both.
  3. Parts of one whole (dollhouse bedroom + kitchen): complement.
  4. Home decor of different kinds: complement only if the names show a shared style, motif or collection; otherwise not a bundle.
  5. Food: complement needs a concrete meal, recipe or use (rolls + sausage; coffee + milk). “Both are groceries” is not enough.
  6. A product that also contains the other (mug + mug with spoon): substitute.
  7. A carrier or shopping bag sold as a designed product (jumbo bag with a pattern) is a product: apply the other rules.
  8. A vague category (“other vegetables”) is judged by what it plausibly covers.

How sure

Clear = you’d bet on it. Probably = leaning. Guess = could go either way. When it isn’t clear, write a few words why.
