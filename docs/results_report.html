Computational Linguistics × Small Local LLM (gemma4:e2b-it-qat)

Rules and a small LLM beat either one alone—and deterministic checks nearly double publishable flyer copy.

Pooled over 324 held-out product pairs, combining a computational-linguistics cascade with a 4.6B local LLM raises bundle F1 from 0.51 (LLM alone) and 0.49 (rules alone) to 0.62 while cutting LLM calls by 30%. On 270 multilingual flyer texts, deterministic checks + targeted repair lift publishable copy from 27% to 50%. 5. Honest Limits, Disclosures & Reproducibility

  • Silver-Standard AI Annotations & New Human Audit Tool:
    • Gold bundle labels (eval/gold/final_*.csv) and blind copy ratings were produced by two large LLMs from the same family under a blind protocol, not human retail merchandisers.
    • To audit human-vs-silver agreement directly, run the new CLI: .venv/bin/python experiments/human_audit.py --sample and --interactive / --score.
  • Pair-Level Independence vs. Item-Level Resampling:
    • Candidate rules share products (e.g. multiple rules containing Whole Milk in t2). Under item-level block bootstrap resampling, the main cascade gains (CL − L on F1 and Precision) remain strictly positive, whereas CL − C (H3) and Lf − L (H4) widen across zero.
  • Domain Shift & Constructed German Data:
    • t1 shares UCI vocabulary with the tuning set (most flattering F1: 0.70), t2 uses coarse category labels from R's Groceries dataset where head-noun rules barely fire (7% call savings, best F1: 0.41), and t3 is a constructed German shelf benchmark rather than organic receipt logs.
  • Separation of Frozen Study vs. Working Product Pipeline:
    • All study tables above come from the untouched frozen snapshot experiments/frozen/export_smart_flyer_v1.py (SHA-256 5e58efdeceb8ec1d…, verified by 594 checks in experiments/verify/verify_numbers.py).
    • All four 2026-11-11 improvements live in the working product pipeline (pipeline/export_smart_flyer.py, pipeline/copy_checks.py, public/*.json, and src/components/SmartFlyerShowcase.tsx) and are covered by 128 passing unit tests.
